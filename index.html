HOW TO PLAY

  • NEAR MISS — brush past cars for bonus points. Within 0.35 m is a PERFECT (x2).
  • COMBO — chain near misses within 3 s to raise your multiplier.
  • KEEP LEFT / KEEP RIGHT — Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand drive on the left; Vietnam, the Philippines, Cambodia and Myanmar drive on the right. Check the sign at each border gate.
  • WRONG WAY — riding in the oncoming lanes scores x2. Watch out for head-on traffic.
  • JUMPS — ride up a ramp truck or a gold-striped jump ramp. In the air, throttle / brake = FLIP (+500), steer = 360 SPIN (+1,200), both at once = DIAGONAL MEGA FLIP (+3,000) per rotation. Land level and you are invincible for a moment; land crooked and you crash.
  • SQUALL — 30 s of heavy rain (score x1.3). Puddles make you slide.
  • Avoid roadside stalls, center-line cones and road signs, and cars drifting over the center line.
  • POWER-UPS (every 100-150 m) — Shield, Magnet, Giant Mode (huge and unstoppable), x2 Score, Slow-Mo.
  • ROAD HAZARDS — potholes slow you down; open manholes and fallen cargo boxes end your run.
  • DROP ZONE — ride straight through the glowing green zone at the road edge for +30 coins.
  • NATIONAL TEAM BIKE — be #1 in your country to ride it. Lose #1 and it is taken back.
