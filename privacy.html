Privacy Policy

Grub Rider · Last updated: 2026-11-01

This policy explains what information Grub Rider ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. Grub Rider is operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. Information we collect

  • Account — the user ID and nickname you choose, and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our backend provider). We do not ask for your real name, phone number or email address.
  • Game data — coins, owned and equipped items, best score and distance, missions, daily streak and settings, saved to your account so your progress follows you.
  • Leaderboard — your nickname, score, distance, equipped items and country are shown publicly to other players.
  • Country — when you first play, your country is estimated from your IP address using third-party IP lookup services (country.is, GeoJS, ipapi.co), with your device time zone as a fallback. Only the two-letter country code is stored; the IP address itself is not stored by us.
  • Purchases — if you buy coins, the app store (Google Play or the App Store) processes the payment. We receive the product, transaction ID and platform through our payment provider (RevenueCat) to deliver your coins. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Gameplay statistics — after each run we record distance, score, coins earned, run length, the bike used, the map country and the app build, linked to your account (or anonymous for guests). We use this to balance the game and measure how it is played.
  • Error reports — if the game hits a technical error, the error message, technical stack trace, app build and a short device description (platform and OS version) are sent so we can fix it.
  • Support messages — if you contact us through Settings → Help & Support, we store your messages, the category you chose, your language, app build and device description, plus our replies. To read and answer messages in your language, we translate support messages and our replies with machine translation services (Google Translate, with MyMemory as a backup). Only the message text is sent to these services, without your account details. Notices are translated the same way when no translation is provided.
  • Language — the language you choose in Settings is saved with your account.
  • Advertising — if rewarded ads are enabled, the ad network may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data under its own privacy policy. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.

2. How we use it

To run the game and save your progress, show leaderboards and country rankings, answer your support requests, understand how the game is played, award the National Team package to the #1 player of each country, deliver purchased coins, prevent cheating and abuse, and fix bugs. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers

Supabase (account and game data hosting), RevenueCat and the app stores (purchases), IP lookup services (country detection), and the ad network if ads are enabled. Data may be processed outside your country, including in Singapore, the United States or the EU.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, game data, leaderboard records, purchase records, gameplay statistics and support messages from our database. Error reports are kept without your account link. Purchased coins are lost when the account is deleted. If you cannot access the app, email us and we will delete your account.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Indonesia's PDP Law, Thailand's PDPA, Vietnam's Decree 13, Singapore's PDPA, the Philippines' Data Privacy Act or Malaysia's PDPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent. Contact us to make a request.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by database access rules so each player can only read and change their own data. No method of storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be shown in the game.
